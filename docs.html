Welcome

JOB MASTER Advanced is a pretend-job game made by The Dennesley Square. You play as a BOSS who hires and pays people, or as an employee who works, earns and moves up. Everything in the game is fictional, and the money is game money only.

This page explains how the game works. Anyone can read it, and you do not need an account.

Heads up: the game is being built in stages. The status table near the end of this page shows what is already live and what is coming soon.

Getting started

You cannot sign yourself up. Every account is created by a BOSS, so the game stays friendly and everyone knows who is who.

  1. Apply. Press Apply now on the home page.
  2. Interview. A BOSS fills in your job application while they interview you.
  3. Get hired. The BOSS sets your salary and registers your account with a special username and password.
  4. Log in. Use the username and password your BOSS gave you.
  5. Start working. Earn, save, pay and climb the ranks.

Keep your username and password private. If you lose them, ask your BOSS to reset your password.

Each person in the game has a role. The role decides which pages and buttons you see.

Money: CRUMPS and CRIPPLES

The game has two currencies.

CRUMPSPaper notes. The bigger currency.
CRIPPLESCoins. The smaller currency.
100 CRIPPLES = 1 CRUMP
So 250 CRIPPLES is 2.5 CRUMPS, and 3 CRUMPS is 300 CRIPPLES.

This money has no value outside the game. You cannot buy it with real money, and you cannot cash it out.

Jobs and salaries

A job starts with an application. The BOSS fills it in during your interview and sets your salary.

  • Salaries are paid only when the BOSS or the Bank Manager presses Pay. Nothing is paid automatically.
  • Salary money comes out of the bank. At the start, the bank holds about 7 million CRUMPS and the BOSS owns 1 million CRUMPS.
  • If the bank does not have enough, the payment does not go through.
  • Every payment is recorded, so you can always see where money came from and where it went.

Cards and QR payments

JOB MASTER has its own card system. It works like a card machine in a shop.

  1. The BOSS creates your card. Your card has a QR code and a barcode that point to your account.
  2. The seller opens the scanner in the game and points the camera at your code.
  3. The game finds your card and asks for the password (PIN).
  4. You type your password. If it is right and you have enough money, the payment goes through.

Good to know

  • The code only identifies your card. It never contains your password.
  • The camera turns on only when you open the scanner, and your phone or browser asks for permission first.
  • Never share your password, even with a friend. Nobody needs it to take a payment from you.

Transfers and banking

You will be able to send money to other players and keep an eye on your own account, including your balance in both currencies and a history of your payments. This is part of the banking and account system.

Questions and answers

Is this real money?

No. CRUMPS and CRIPPLES are game money. They cannot be bought with real money or turned into real money.

Can I make my own account?

No. A BOSS creates every account after your interview. Press Apply now to start.

I forgot my password. What now?

Ask your BOSS to reset it. There is no email recovery, because accounts are not tied to an email address.

Does the game watch me through my camera?

No. The camera is only used to scan a card, and only after you open the scanner and allow it. You can say no.

Which devices does it work on?

Phones, tablets and computers. The pages are made to fit every screen.

Who made the game?

The Dennesley Square.
