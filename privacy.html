The short version

  • We collect only what the game needs to work: your username, a scrambled version of your password, and your game activity.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads from outside companies.
  • Please do not put real personal details into the game.
  • The camera is used only to scan cards, only after you allow it.

This policy covers the JOB MASTER Advanced website and the game accounts as they roll out. The public pages do not ask you for any personal information.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Username and password (the password is stored scrambled, never as plain text)So you can log in. Your BOSS creates these for you.
Job application details your BOSS enters during your interviewTo set up your job, role and salary. Only what the game form asks for.
Game activity: balances in CRUMPS and CRIPPLES, payments, transfers, titles, packagesTo run the game and keep an honest record of every payment.
Card details: a random card code, which is what the QR code and barcode containTo recognise your card when it is scanned. The code never contains your password.
Basic technical data, such as your device type, browser and IP address, which our hosting service may record in its logsTo keep the site working and secure.

What we do not collect

  • No real-money payment details. The game has no real-money payments.
  • No email address or phone number is needed to play.
  • No photos or video from your camera are saved or uploaded.
  • No precise location.
Please keep it pretend. Do not type your real name, address, phone number, school, or ID numbers into the game. Use a game name instead.

Camera

The scanner uses your camera to read a QR code or barcode. Your phone or browser asks for permission first, and you can say no. The picture is read on your own device to find the card code, and the camera stops when you close the scanner. We do not record or store camera images.

Cookies and browser storage

When you log in, the game keeps a small login token in your browser so you stay logged in. It may also remember simple things such as a setting. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who can see your information

  • You can see your own account, balance and payment history.
  • Your BOSS and the Bank Manager can see what they need to run the game: your application, salary, card and payments.
  • The Owner can see game records to fix problems and enforce the rules.
  • Other players can see only what the game shows publicly, such as your username and title.

We do not sell or rent your information.

Services that help us run the game

We use outside services to host the website and to store game data. These services process data for us and have their own privacy policies. The pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page opens. We will list the hosting and database services here once they are final.

How long we keep information

We keep your account and game records while your account is active, because payment history needs to stay accurate. If an account is closed, we delete or anonymise the personal parts, although payment records may be kept in a form that no longer points to a person so the game's books still add up.

Your choices

  • Ask your BOSS to reset your password at any time.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • Say no to the camera. You can still play, although you cannot scan cards.

Contact options are listed at the bottom of this page.

Keeping information safe

Passwords are stored scrambled, the site is served over a secure connection, and the game checks every payment on the server so it cannot be changed from a browser. No system is perfect, so please choose a password you do not use anywhere else and keep your card password private.

Young players

Accounts are created by a BOSS who knows the player, and the game asks for as little personal information as possible. We ask parents, guardians and BOSSes to look out for younger players and to keep real personal details out of the game.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game grows. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or want to see or delete your information? Ask your BOSS, or contact The Dennesley Square through the contact option that will be added to the game. See also our Terms of Service.
