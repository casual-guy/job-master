About these terms

These terms explain the rules for using the JOB MASTER Advanced website and game, made by The Dennesley Square. By using the site or logging in to a game account, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

We wrote them in plain language on purpose.

The game is pretend

  • JOB MASTER Advanced is a game. Jobs, companies, salaries and cards are fictional.
  • CRUMPS and CRIPPLES are game money. They have no real-world value.
  • You cannot buy game money with real money, sell it, or swap it for real money, goods or services. Please do not try to trade game items or accounts for real money.
  • Premium packages are bought only with game money.

Accounts

  • Accounts are created by a BOSS after an application and interview. You cannot create an account yourself.
  • Your username and password belong to you alone. Do not share them or let someone else play as you.
  • You are responsible for what happens on your account. If you think someone else knows your password, tell your BOSS straight away so it can be reset.
  • Keep your card password (PIN) private. Nobody needs it except you.

BOSS and Bank Manager powers

BOSSes and Bank Managers have extra tools, such as creating accounts, setting salaries, paying salaries, issuing cards and resetting passwords. They must use them fairly and only for the game. They are players too, not staff of The Dennesley Square, and can be removed from their role by the Owner at any time.

All payments are recorded. The Owner may look at these records to settle a problem or to check that the rules are followed.

Fair play

To keep the game fun for everyone, please do not:

  • cheat, or use bugs, tricks or tools to create money or items you did not earn;
  • try to get into someone else's account, or guess passwords or PINs;
  • attack, overload or try to break the site, or look for ways around its protections;
  • scan or pay with a card that is not yours;
  • run more than one account unless the Owner has allowed it.

If you find a bug, please report it instead of using it. We are happy to hear about problems.

Behaviour and content

Be kind. Do not use names, titles, advertisements or any other text in the game to harass people, share hate, share adult content, or break the law. Do not put real personal details, such as your home address, phone number or ID numbers, into the game. We may remove content that breaks these rules.

Premium packages

Gold, Platinum and Diamond packages give the benefits listed on the home page and in the documentation, such as extra advertisements and bonus CRUMPS per salary income. We may change what each package includes, or its price in game money, to keep the game balanced. Because packages are bought with game money only, there are no real-money refunds.

Availability and changes to the game

We try to keep the game running, but we do not promise it will always be available or free of errors. The game is provided as is. We may update, pause, reset or end parts of the game, including balances, titles and packages, to fix problems or to improve it. We will try to give notice of big changes on the site.

Suspending or ending accounts

The Owner may warn, suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms, and may reverse payments that came from cheating. You can ask your BOSS or the Owner to close your account at any time.

Limits on our responsibility

Because the game is for fun and has no real money, we are not responsible for losses of game money, titles or items. To the extent the law allows, The Dennesley Square is not liable for any indirect or incidental harm that comes from using the site. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have by law that cannot be removed.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the game grows. The date at the top of this page shows when they last changed. If you keep using the site after a change, it means you accept the new terms.

Contact

Questions about these terms? Ask your BOSS, or contact The Dennesley Square through the contact option that will be added to the game. See also our Privacy Policy.
